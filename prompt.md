# Weekly welcome agent: the prompt

This is the instruction the scheduled Claude task runs every week.
Replace the group URL and texts with your own.

---

Weekly welcome post for the Facebook group "קלוד בלי קוד לעסקים צומחים".
Work in Chrome on the group admin's computer (Claude in Chrome).

1. Download the welcome image from
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zarmumia/group-welcome-agent/main/assets/welcome.png
   into the workspace.
2. Open https://www.facebook.com/groups/claudeblikod/people/ and read the members list.
   Collect members who joined in the last 7 days (skip admins).
   Check the latest welcome post in the group so nobody is welcomed twice.
3. If there are no new members: do nothing in the group, and notify the admin
   "אין מצטרפים חדשים השבוע".
4. Otherwise open the group's "Write welcome post" composer (it tags new members automatically).
   If it is not offered, open a regular post and tag each new member with @.
   - First line: "ברוכים הבאים לחברים החדשים שלנו 👋"
   - Then the tagged names.
   - Last line: "איזה כיף שהצטרפתם! אשמח שתספרו בכמה מילים: מה העסק שלכם, ומה הדבר האחד שהכי הייתם רוצים שירוץ לבד?"
   - Attach the welcome image.
5. Do NOT click "Post". Leave the composer open and notify the admin (in Hebrew):
   who joined this week, and that the post is ready for her to publish.

Rules: never publish on the admin's behalf, never message members, never change group settings.
